## Provenance & Event: data, not the serializer

The profile stays undeclared. Counted read-only on 27 November 2026, of 247 activities:

| Type | Activities | With a participant | With a result | With a start or end date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Production | 229 | 58 | 208 | 182 |
| Accumulation | 10 | 7 | 7 | 9 |
| Reproduction | 4 | 0 | 2 | 4 |
| Custody | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| No type | 3 | 0 | 0 | 2 |

: Activities and what the Provenance & Event profile asks of them

The serializer reads participants from `performed_by` and results from `results_from`, with the activity as subject. Only three relations hold the same facts in another form (one `performedActivity` and one `resultedFrom` with the activity as object, and one `results_from` the wrong way round), and eight relations make an activity the creator or accumulator of something, which is a data error. AtoM's own creation events do not fill the gap: of 133, 11 name an agent. So the gap is in the data: 171 productions name no participant at all. Declaring the profile needs that data, not a serializer change.

## Identifier sweep

Every kind of identifier the service emits was followed, read-only, as a data client and as a browser. All nine entity kinds (record, agent, repository, place, rule, activity, function, instantiation) answer JSON-LD whose `@id` is the identifier, and a browser reaches the viewer. The openric.org namespace and vocabulary pages answer HTML only, as GitHub Pages cannot negotiate content; from spec v0.61.6 each links its Turtle as an alternate.
